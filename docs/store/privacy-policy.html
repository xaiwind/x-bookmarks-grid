X Bookmarks Grid — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

X Bookmarks Grid ("the extension") is a browser extension that syncs your own X (Twitter) bookmarks into your browser and lets you browse, search and filter them. It is a third-party tool and is not affiliated with X Corp.

We do not collect any data

The extension has no server. It does not send any of your data to the developer or to any third party, and it contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

What the extension processes on your device

To work, the extension reads and stores the following in your browser:

  • Details of posts you bookmarked: link, author handle and display name, title (the beginning of the post or the article title), cover image links, external links, publication time, view count and post type.
  • What you enter in the extension: notes, edited titles and deletion marks.
  • Settings and sync state: the automatic sync switch and time, and the time and progress of the last sync.

All of this is kept in the local storage of your current browser profile (IndexedDB and chrome.storage). The extension does not store post text. When you choose "Download", it temporarily reads that post's text and writes it into the Markdown file you download; the text is not kept in the extension. If "Also save photos and videos locally" is on, it downloads that post's photos and videos from X's media servers (pbs.twimg.com, video.twimg.com) into the folder you chose.

How the extension accesses X

  • The extension uses the X session you are already signed in to in your browser, and reads your own bookmark list and the posts you point it at from within X's pages. These requests go only to X's website (x.com / twitter.com).
  • The extension does not read or store your password or cookies, and it never posts, likes, follows or changes your bookmarks on your behalf.
  • The extension does not access any site other than X. Cover images are loaded by your browser directly from X's image servers.

Retention and deletion

  • Data stays on your device until you delete it.
  • You can delete individual bookmarks in the extension, or use "Erase local data and rebuild".
  • Removing the extension from your browser's extensions page deletes all data it stored.
  • Files created by "Export backup" are kept by you; the extension does not upload them.

Sharing

The extension does not sell, transfer or share your data with anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page is updated and the change is noted in the release notes of the new version.

X 收藏瀑布流 隐私权政策

最后更新：2026 年 11 月 19 日

X 收藏瀑布流（以下简称“本插件”）是一个浏览器扩展，用来把你自己在 X（Twitter）上的收藏同步到你的浏览器本地，并提供浏览、搜索和筛选。本插件是第三方工具，与 X Corp. 没有关联。

我们不收集任何数据

本插件没有服务器。它不会把你的任何数据发送给开发者或任何第三方，不含统计、广告或跟踪代码。

本插件在你的设备上处理哪些数据

为了实现功能，本插件会在你的浏览器里读取并保存以下内容：

  • 你收藏的帖子的信息：链接、作者用户名和显示名、标题（帖子开头的文字或文章标题）、封面图片链接、外部链接、发布时间、浏览量、帖子类型。
  • 你在插件里填写的内容：备注、修改过的标题、删除标记。
  • 插件设置和同步状态：自动同步开关与时间、上次同步时间和进度。

这些数据全部保存在你当前浏览器配置的本地存储（IndexedDB 和 chrome.storage）中。本插件不保存帖子正文。当你选择“下载文章”时，插件会临时读取该帖子的正文并写入你下载的 Markdown 文件，正文不会留在插件里。如果你开启了“同时把图片和视频存到本地”，插件会从 X 的图片和视频服务器（pbs.twimg.com、video.twimg.com）下载这条帖子的图片和视频，保存到你选择的文件夹。

本插件如何访问 X

  • 本插件使用你浏览器中已登录的 X 会话，在 X 的页面内读取你自己的收藏列表和你指定的帖子。相关请求只发往 X 的网站（x.com / twitter.com）。
  • 本插件不读取、不保存你的密码或 Cookie，也不会替你发帖、点赞、关注或修改收藏。
  • 本插件不访问 X 以外的任何网站。封面图片由浏览器直接从 X 的图片服务器加载。

数据的保留与删除

  • 数据一直保存在你的设备上，直到你删除它。
  • 你可以在插件里删除单条收藏，或使用“清空本地数据并重建”。
  • 在浏览器的扩展程序页面移除本插件，会同时删除它保存的全部数据。
  • “导出备份”生成的文件由你自己保管，本插件不会上传它。

数据共享

本插件不出售、不转让、不与任何人共享你的数据。

政策变更

如果本政策有变化，会更新本页面顶部的日期，并在插件的新版本说明中提示。
